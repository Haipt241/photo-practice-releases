Data processed on your device

The app reads images you explicitly select through the Android document picker, creates private JPEG copies for composition practice, and reads EXIF orientation to display them correctly. These copies are re-encoded; GPS/EXIF metadata from the original is not copied into them. Original images outside the app are not modified or deleted.

Photos captured in Photo Practice are crops of practice scenes or imported images, not photos taken with a physical camera. The app stores PNG images, capture times, dimensions, crop regions, composition/effect settings, source information and integrity hashes in its private storage. A hash is not encryption. Interface preferences are stored on your device.

Collection, sharing and permissions

The current version has no accounts, advertising, in-app purchases, analytics SDKs, remote crash-reporting SDKs or online AI image generation. The app has no Internet permission and does not send photos or usage data to the publisher's servers. It does not request camera, location or broad photo-library access; it only reads files you select.

The Android document picker may display cloud providers you have configured. Any file download by such a provider is subject to that provider's policies. If you choose to open a source or license link in an external browser, the website provider may process data under its own policies. Android and Google Play also operate under their own policies.

Retention, deletion and backup

Data remains until you clear the app's data in Android Settings or uninstall the app. This removes imported copies, the private album and preferences; original images outside the app are not deleted. Keep important originals. The app asks Android to exclude its data from Android backup and device-transfer mechanisms; manufacturers' separate copying tools may behave differently.

Phone gallery export and album deletion

You may select captures or save all captures to Pictures/Photo Practice in the phone gallery. This is a local copy, not cloud synchronization. On Android 9 and earlier only, saving requires storage permission; newer Android versions do not require broad library access. Deleting a capture in the app removes its private image and metadata, but does not delete gallery copies. Gallery copies can remain after uninstalling and may be backed up by a gallery or cloud service you configured. Manage those copies in that service or your phone gallery.

Data protection and changes

Data is stored in Android's application sandbox. No system can guarantee absolute security, particularly on a compromised device. If data-processing features change, this policy and the store declarations must be updated before the new features are released.

Optional Ko-fi support

The Buy me a coffee button opens https://ko-fi.com/haipt35 in an external browser after your confirmation. Contributions to H&H studio are voluntary and do not unlock app content or features. The app does not embed a payment SDK, collect payment details, or verify contributions. Ko-fi, its payment providers and your browser may process information under their own privacy policies. Review those policies before paying.

Rating reminder

The app locally stores a successful capture count and whether it has shown the rating reminder, to offer it once after 10 photos. These values are not transmitted. If you choose to rate, the app opens Google Play; reviews you submit are handled under Google Play policies. The app does not read your star rating or check whether you submitted a review.

Intended audience

Photo Practice is designed for adults aged 18 and over. It is not designed or marketed as a child-directed app. The app does not request or store a date of birth. Support through Ko-fi is optional and does not affect access to app features.
