The default library contains 40 pre-generated AI scenes: 10 environments without people and 30 fictional model-pose variants. The app does not generate AI images during use and does not send personal photos to AI services.

These are fictional practice scenes, not documentary photographs of actual people, places or events. Similarities may be coincidental; geometry, text, faces and other details may be inaccurate.

Captured images are crops of source pixels. Zoom does not increase native resolution. An AI label is not a copyright certificate or a guarantee of exclusive ownership; usage rights depend on applicable law and the image-generation service's terms.

Images you choose to import are labeled separately as device imports and are not automatically described as AI images generated by Photo Practice.
